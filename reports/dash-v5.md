## 1 · The tab group is centred on the bar, and the tabs are bigger

`web/apps/dashboard/src/shell.css`, `web/apps/dashboard/src/main.tsx`.

**Centring.** The wordmark and a new `.topbar-actions` group are both `flex:1 1 0` with a
`min-width:max-content` floor. Equal flex-basis and equal grow means the free space is
split equally between them, so the two side groups come out the *same width* — which is
what puts the tab group's centre on the bar's centre, rather than merely between the two
groups. Neither side may shrink past its own content, so when ten tabs no longer fit the
tab row is the only thing that gives: it then spans the whole gap between the groups, an
equal gutter from each, and scrolls.

**Size.** Icon 22 px at every width (was 17 px), tab height the bar's full 64 px (was
60 px), label 15 px and padding 14 px where they fit. Ten tabs cost **699.8 px before any
padding** at a 15 px label — 479.8 px of label plus 22 px of icon each — measured in the
browser, and true centring needs `2 × wordmark + row + gutters ≤ viewport`. So the bar
sheds in a fixed order as it narrows: the app name beside the wordmark, the wordmark's
height, the bar's padding, the gap between groups, and last the tab's own padding.

| ≥ 1680 px | 24 | 14 | logo 15 px + "Signals" (234 px) | 15 px | 22 px | 14 px | 1049.7 px |
| 1440–1679 | 20 | 12 | logo 14 px (154 px) | 15 px | 22 px | 12 px | 999.7 px |

### Two deviations, both forced by arithmetic, both measured

- **The label is 13 px below 1280 px, not 15 px.** At 1024 px the bar has 992 px of
  content box. Ten tabs at a 15 px label cost 699.8 px *with no padding at all*; a
  legible wordmark is 132 px and the action group 34 px, with 20 px of gutters. That is
  885.8 px before a single pixel of tab padding, leaving 106 px — about 5 px of padding
  per side, with the tabs touching. The label keeps its previous 13 px there instead, so
  the icon still grows (17 → 22 px, the brief's "visibly larger on every screen") and ten
  tabs fit with nothing scrolling. 15 px holds at 1280 px and above, where the brief's
  measurements are taken.
- **The app name "Signals" beside the wordmark shows only at ≥ 1680 px.** Centring the
  row needs twice the left group inside the viewport: with the full 234 px brand and the
  1049.7 px row that is 1593.8 px, so below ~1600 px the app name is what the bar sheds
  first. The wordmark itself (the mcpramen logo) is present at every width, and visual QA
  still asserts it on every screen.

The brief also describes the bar's right group as "theme/sign-out". Sign-out is not in
the top bar in this build — it sits in the workspace strip beside the freshness badge —
and this pass did not move it. The right group is the theme toggle and the mobile-menu
button, both inside the new `.topbar-actions`.

## 2 · The whole page loads and animates on open; nothing waits for scrolling

New module `web/packages/ui/src/motion.ts`; `charts/index.tsx`, `analytics-v3.tsx`,
`styles.css`.

Two `IntersectionObserver`s are gone: the one in `Chart` that refused to build a chart's
SVG until the figure entered the viewport, and the one in `Card` that held a card's
entrance until the same. Everything on a screen is now built and animated at mount.

**The choreography**, one run per navigation:

| what | timing |
|---|---|
| context / heading / range | 40 / 90 / 140 ms (shell CSS, unchanged) |
| tiles | 130 + i·55 ms, 520 ms each |
| cards | 300 + i·85 ms capped at 1100 ms, 520 ms each |
| chart draw-in | its card's or tile's entrance + 180 ms |
| count-ups | 640 ms from the tile's own delay |

**The longest-task gate is kept** by queuing chart SVG construction one card per
animation frame, in document order (`queuePaint`). The visual order is unchanged — the
card is still invisible when its chart is built — and no single task carries a whole
screen's SVG construction.

**A chart's draw-in takes two forms, so two clocks.** The parts a renderer builds
(`.plot`, `.stroke-draw`, `.growx`, `.growy`) are marked `on` when the SVG is built, some
frames after the mount, and carry `--cd`: what is *left* of their card's entrance at that
moment, added to the renderer's own per-element stagger
(`animation-delay:calc(var(--cd,0ms) + var(--pd,0ms))`). The boxes that animate from the
first render — a tile's sparkline wrapper, the stacked area's own box, the donut's own
box — carry `--draw-in`, the same instant measured from the mount. Both land on
`ownerStart + 180 ms`. Without this second clock those three animations still started at
the mount, ahead of their card; `choreography-qa.mjs` caught it.

**One run per navigation.** Nothing on scroll (no observers left), nothing on resize (a
repaint at a new width lands finished, `nt`), nothing on a poll refresh (the v4 rule,
still asserted by `live-qa.mjs`). Reduced motion shows the finished page at once.

**QA.** Every "scroll into view before capture" step is out of `visual-qa.mjs`:
`settleCharts` waits for the queue instead of scrolling, the manual top-to-bottom scroll
sweep is gone, and each screen now asserts that with `scrollY === 0` every chart has its
SVG and its table twin, and that no full-page capture would show an unpainted card. New
gate `scripts/choreography-qa.mjs` (wired into `scripts/test.mjs`) reads every entrance
and draw-in from the animations themselves and holds them to the table above, proves
nothing runs on scroll or resize, and records a 100 ms-step screenshot series into
`web/artifacts/v5/choreography/`.

## 3 · The stray line in the tool drawer

`web/packages/ui/src/styles.css`.

The only full-width horizontal rule in the upper part of either drawer is the drawer
header's `border-bottom`, measured at y = 94 with the tiles row starting at y = 94, so it
reads as a line drawn across the top of the three tiles. Nothing else in this design
separates a title from its own content with a line, so the header keeps its 18 px of
padding and loses the border: `.drawer .drawer-header{border-bottom:0}`. The session
drawer shares the same header component and is covered by the same rule; its only other
full-width border is the `.table-scroll-hint` above a scrollable chart, which is a real
affordance and stays.

## 4 · Drawers must not blur the page behind them

`web/packages/ui/src/styles.css`, `web/apps/dashboard/src/shell.css`.

```css
.drawer::backdrop{background:color-mix(in oklch,var(--ink-hard) 28%,transparent);backdrop-filter:none}
.dark .drawer::backdrop{background:color-mix(in oklch,oklch(0.220 0.013 70) 40%,transparent)}
html:has(dialog[open]) .topbar{backdrop-filter:none;background:var(--background)}
```

The unused `.scrim` class in the stylesheet gets the same treatment, so no rule in the
shipped CSS asks for a backdrop filter behind a dialog.

**One judgement call, flagged.** The brief asks for "`--ink-hard` at 28 % in light, 40 %
in dark". In light `--ink-hard` is `var(--foreground)`, a near-black, and 28 % of it is
the scrim the brief describes. In dark, `.dark` *remaps* `--ink-hard` to
`oklch(0.845 0.152 82)` — the gold it draws focal-card edges and the design-note rule
with — so `color-mix(--ink-hard 40%)` there would put a bright gold haze over the page.
The dark rule names the same hard ink the light theme resolves `--ink-hard` to, at the
40 % the brief asks for. Measured: `oklch(0.22 0.013 70 / 0.28)` light,
`oklch(0.22 0.013 70 / 0.4)` dark, `backdrop-filter: none` on both.

With both drawers open, in both themes, the number of elements in the document with a
computed `backdrop-filter` other than `none` is **0**, and the drawer header's
`border-bottom-width` is `0px`:

```
tool/light:    backdrop=oklch(0.22 0.013 70 / 0.28) filter=none headBorder=0px none topbarFilter=none filtered=[]
session/light: backdrop=oklch(0.22 0.013 70 / 0.28) filter=none headBorder=0px none topbarFilter=none filtered=[]
tool/dark:     backdrop=oklch(0.22 0.013 70 / 0.4)  filter=none headBorder=0px none topbarFilter=none filtered=[]
session/dark:  backdrop=oklch(0.22 0.013 70 / 0.4)  filter=none headBorder=0px none topbarFilter=none filtered=[]
```

The top bar paints its own opaque background while a dialog is open rather than staying
74 % translucent with no blur, which would show the scrolled page through it. That is the
one visible consequence of removing the filter, and it is what makes the capture
deterministic: `mask:[page.locator('.topbar')]` is out of both drawer captures in
`visual-qa.mjs`, and the four serial static runs below are byte-identical without it.

## Divergences found, not fixed

- **The mobile-menu button is `display:none` at every width.** The v3 glass-shell
  override (`.mobile-menu{display:none}`, later in the stylesheet than the
  `@media(max-width:1250px)` rule that shows it) has hidden it since v3, so the stacked
  menu is unreachable in this build and the tab row scrolls instead at narrow widths —
  which is what the brief asks for below 1024 px. This pass fixed the stacked list's own
  layout (it was clipped to one row by the bar's height) but did not re-enable the
  button: doing so would have to hide the tab row at those widths, which contradicts
  "ten tabs must fit" and the visual-QA assertion that ten animated nav icons render at
  320 px.
- **Sign-out is not in the top bar.** The brief describes the bar's right group as
  "theme/sign-out"; in this build sign-out is a button in the workspace strip. Left where
  it is.
- **`artifacts/v5/choreography/` is timing-dependent** and deliberately outside the
  byte-identity gate, exactly as `artifacts/v4/reload/` is: a 100 ms screenshot series of
  a running animation cannot be byte-identical between runs. The assertions in
  `choreography-qa.mjs` are what gate it; the frames are evidence.
- **`ci-conformance.py` serves whatever is in `web/dist` and does not rebuild it** — the
  same note the v4 report made. `bun run build` immediately before it is the order the
  acceptance list already gives, and is what was done here.
